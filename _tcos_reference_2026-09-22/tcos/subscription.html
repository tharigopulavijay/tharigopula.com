Payment opens on Razorpay's secure page. TCOS never sees or stores your card, UPI mandate or bank details. You can stop a renewal at any time and keep your access until the period you have paid for ends.

If a renewal fails

Your clinic keeps its paid access for three days while Razorpay retries and tells you what needs attention. If it is still unpaid after that, TCOS moves to Free. Records are retained and paid access returns as soon as payment succeeds.
