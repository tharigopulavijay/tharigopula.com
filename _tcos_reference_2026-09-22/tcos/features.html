What it does

Everything the clinic runs on

Built around a clinic with one doctor and two or three staff — which is most clinics, and almost nobody builds for it.

📅Appointments & the day

  • Today's diary with arrivals and waiting
  • Walk-ins added by the front desk in seconds
  • Follow-ups scheduled from the visit itself
  • Appointment requests from your public page

👤Patients

  • Your own patient numbering
  • Households: one mobile, separate people
  • Full history on one screen
  • Search by name, number or any digits of the mobile

📜Prescriptions

  • Sections that match your discipline
  • Numbered and frozen once issued
  • Mistakes amended with a reason, never quietly edited
  • Medicine catalogue as a typing aid, across all systems

🏥Pharmacy

  • Batch and expiry tracking
  • First-expiry-first-out dispensing
  • Expired stock quarantined, never sold
  • Low stock and near-expiry warnings

₹Billing

  • Gap-free numbered invoices
  • Your own fee list
  • Cash, card and UPI recorded against the bill
  • Part payments and outstanding tracked

👥Your team

  • Tick exactly what each person may do
  • One person can run front desk and pharmacy
  • No member of staff can open a clinical record
  • Every change to a record logged

📊Reports

  • What the practice earned, by day and month
  • What you treated most
  • Stock movement and pharmacy value
  • Follow-ups nobody came back for

🌐Your patients' view

  • A page for your clinic with timings and services
  • Patients open their record from a link you send
  • Prescriptions, advice and lab results, readable
  • Read-only: nobody can edit their own record
On your phone

It installs like an app

TCOS runs in the browser, so there is nothing to download from a store and nothing to update. Add it to your home screen and it opens full screen with its own icon, the way any other app does — on the phone, the tablet at the front desk, or the computer in your room.

Add it now, before you even have an account. It opens at the sign-in screen and waits for you.
