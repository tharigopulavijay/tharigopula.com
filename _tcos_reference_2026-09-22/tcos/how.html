Getting started

Why we do not let you sign up instantly

A prescription issued through TCOS carries our name as well as yours. So we check that every doctor on the platform is a doctor — it takes us a day or two, and it is the reason your patients can trust what they receive.

You apply

One short form, in three steps. Your registration number and council are what we check, so those matter most.

We verify

We confirm your registration against the council register and call you on the number you gave.

We activate

We set up your clinic on the right product and send your sign-in details on that call.

You change the password

First sign-in forces a new password only you know. Then add your team and start.

Before you ask

The three questions every doctor asks us

What does it cost to start?

Nothing. There is no card at any point in this process, and the free plan is a real plan rather than a countdown. Paid plans exist when the clinic outgrows it.

Whose data is it?

Yours. Your patient records belong to your clinic and you can ask us for all of them, at any time, in a form you can actually open.

What if I am already on paper?

Most clinics are. Start with today's patients and let the history build itself — nobody needs to type in four years of files before the first consultation.

Two minutes, and we take it from there. No card, and no account exists until we have verified you.
